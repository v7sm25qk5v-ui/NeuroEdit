# NeuroEdit v0.5.20-alpha

Inserted slides: a slide can take its own time on the timeline so the video
after it plays later, instead of hiding the footage behind it. Retains the
in-app updater and MPG/MPEG import from earlier releases.

## Try it

1. Add a clip, put the playhead at 0:00, open **Slides** and choose **New Slide**.
   The clip now starts after the slide; nothing is hidden.
2. Drag the slide on the timeline: it moves between clips, and the clips part
   around it.
3. Change **Placement** to **Cover video** to lay a slide over footage (the
   hidden footage is hatched), or **Overlay** for text over playing video.

Projects from earlier versions open unchanged: their full slides become
**Cover video** slides. Switch one to **Insert** to bring back footage it hid.

## Included updates

- New **Placement** for slides: Insert (push video later), Cover video, Overlay.
  New Slide inserts by default.
- Inserted slides snap to the nearest clip edge within 2 s (wider when zoomed
  out). Well inside a clip, the playhead splits the clip on purpose.
- Dragging an inserted slide moves it between clips only; Escape cancels.
  Changing its duration, deleting it, or switching it to Cover moves the video
  to match. Each edit is one undo step.
- Timeline: a push icon on inserted slides, a dashed gap in the Video row, and
  hatching over footage a cover slide hides.
- A title slide before the first clip now stays in place when clips are reordered.
- Take Still never lands inside an inserted slide.
- Redactions: one that ends exactly where a slide is inserted between clips stops
  there instead of covering the slide. Inside a clip it still extends over the
  resumed footage. A redaction moved by an insert keeps its tracked-mask frames aligned.

## Validation and limits

The complete local suite passed **804 tests** with a clean exit, including 39
inserted-slide tests (snapping, moves, resizing, redactions and mask frames,
timeline drag and undo, and an export frame comparison). An independent review
ran about 7,000 randomized edit sequences; its findings were fixed before release.

Not yet tried in the running app with real footage. Audio, markers and captions
that start inside an inserted slide do not move with it. At low zoom a short
inserted slide shows only its icon; the title is in the tooltip. This is the
first update delivered through the in-app updater; its install/restart remains
an alpha smoke test. Windows runtime validation limits from v0.5.19-alpha remain.
