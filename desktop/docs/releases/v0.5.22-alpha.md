# NeuroEdit v0.5.22-alpha

Playback no longer freezes at cuts between clips that were split from the same
long recording.

## Smooth cuts

- Clips split from one recording often come from places far apart in that file.
  Imported footage stores a complete picture only every ~8 seconds, so jumping
  there during playback froze the preview for 0.4–0.9 s at each cut.
- A second, hidden player now loads the next clip a few seconds before the cut.
  At the cut the two players swap, and the new picture appears in about 5 ms.
  Your volume and mute settings carry over.
- If the next clip isn't ready in time, NeuroEdit jumps the old way, so the
  worst case is the earlier behavior. Pressing play less than about a second
  before such a cut can still pause briefly at that cut.
- Exports are unchanged; this affects the preview only.
- Retains the Mac in-app update fix and inserted slides from earlier alphas.

## Validation

Local Ruff passed and **831 tests passed**. Eighteen new tests cover when the
next clip is prepared, the player swap, fallbacks and a real-decoder check: a
file with a single keyframe played as two clips 37 s apart changes picture
5–9 ms after the cut, and stalls 0.56 s with the new player disabled. Not yet
tried by hand with real footage in the installed app. Existing informational
Windows test/runtime limits remain.
