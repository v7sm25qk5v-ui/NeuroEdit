# NeuroEdit v0.5.24-alpha.1

Annotation labels fit inside the video frame, and the viewer updates while you
drag the timeline playhead.

- **Labels in the viewer and export** share the same layout. Label boxes
  expand and shift inside the frame, and oversized text wraps or shrinks.
  Selection and dragging follow the fitted box. This fixes labels such as
  “Resection cavity” clipping beside a small shape near the edge.
- **Live scrub previews** decode in the background and prioritize the latest
  pointer position. In the viewer, previews check clip bounds and timed
  redactions. Releasing the mouse seeks to the exact timeline position.
- **Faster filmstrip fill** gives each visible source a first still before
  filling in its strip. Nearby thumbnails reuse decoded frames. Six adjacent
  stills on a synthetic 1080p clip took 0.33 seconds, down from 1.65 seconds.

Synthetic tests covered matching label placement in preview and export,
picture changes while dragging, exact release seeks, and a scrub from
landscape into sparse portrait video. Performance varies with the footage.
Installed Windows playback and playback with actual project footage remain
untested.
