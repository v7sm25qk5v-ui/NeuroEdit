# NeuroEdit v0.5.25-alpha

Change the speed of individual video clips and use refreshed scrollbars and
buttons in light or dark mode.

- Right-click a video clip and choose **Clip Speed**. Pick 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×,
  1.5×, 2×, or 4×. This edit changes the clip's length in preview and export,
  and source audio follows the speed change. The preview speed button still
  controls how fast you watch the timeline.
- Later timeline items move by the change in clip length, and timed annotations
  and redactions move with the footage. Speed changes can be undone.
- Rounded scrollbar rails and thumbs replace the square arrows and blocks.
  Buttons use clearer focus, hover, and pressed states.

Projects containing retimed clips use a newer project format. Open those
projects in this version or later to preserve clip timing and redactions.
